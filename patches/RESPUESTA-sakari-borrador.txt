Yes, exactly the same: on the 7275 the GEXP device declares
OperationRegion (BAR0, SystemMemory, SB04, 0x208) and the I2C4 controller
(INT3446) builds its _CRS from the same SB04 variable, so intel_lpss_acpi
fails with -EBUSY unless acpi_enforce_resources=lax. Thierry's patch 1/8
would fix it here too with a "Latitude 7275" entry added to
intel_lpss_quirk_dmi[]. I'll send that as a small patch on top of his
series (or Thierry, if you prefer to fold it into v11, I'm happy to
provide a Tested-by).

The other 5285 quirk, the static clock consumer list for the broken
INT3479 _DEP, is not needed on the 7275: the tps68470-clk already shows
i2c-INT3479:00 as a consumer.
